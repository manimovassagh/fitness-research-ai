## 💡 Research Standards

Every finding uses a three-level evidence classification:

:::success STRONG Evidence
- Multiple peer-reviewed studies confirm this
- Large sample sizes, good methodology
- Consensus among researchers
- Reliable for decision-making
:::

:::warning MODERATE Evidence
- Some solid research supports this
- More studies needed for certainty
- Probably true, but with caveats
- Consider other factors
:::

:::danger WEAK Evidence
- Limited studies or poor methodology
- Conflicting results
- More research needed
- Don't base major decisions on this alone
:::
